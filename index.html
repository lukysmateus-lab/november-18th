Round 1 • Vault Code

Indefinite Pronouns (SOME / ANY)

  • 1. Choose the Root:
    • SOME- ➔ Affirmative (+) & Polite Offers (Would you like...? / Can I have...?)
    • ANY- ➔ Negative (-) (not, didn't, couldn't, never) & General Questions (?)
  • 2. Choose the Ending:
    • 👤 Person = -one / -body
    • 📦 Object = -thing
    • 📍 Place = -where
Round 2 • Bomb Defusal

Tag Questions (Cut the Right Wire!)

  • 1. Flip the Polarity:
    • Positive sentence (+) ➔ Negative Tag (-)
    • Negative sentence (-) ➔ Positive Tag (+)
  • 2. Match the Verb Tense:
    • Has am/is/are, can, will, have/has? Repeat it!
    • Present Simple action verb? Use don't / doesn't.
    • Past Simple (-ed / irregular)? Use didn't.
Round 3 • Crime Lab

MUST BE, MIGHT BE, CAN'T BE

  • MUST BE (99% True): Strong proof shows it is definitely true. ("Her name is on the cover ➔ It must be hers!")
  • MIGHT BE (50% Maybe): Look for "I'm not sure", "maybe", or "perhaps".
  • CAN'T BE (99% Impossible): Proof shows it is logically impossible!
Round 4 • Laser Mirror

Reflexive Pronouns (Mirror Action)

The subject and the object are the EXACT same person/group!

• I ➔ myself
• You (1) ➔ yourself
• He (Mark) ➔ himself
• She (Lisa) ➔ herself
• We ( X and I ) ➔ ourselves
• You (2+) ➔ yourselves
• They (The kids) ➔ themselves
Round 5 • Final Boss Bridge

EACH OTHER vs. REFLEXIVES (The Ultimate Showdown!)

🤝 EACH OTHER (A ⇄ B Cross Action) Person A does the action to Person B, and Person B does it back to Person A! Ex: The two friends hugged each other, texted each other, and helped each other.
🪞 THEMSELVES / OURSELVES (A ↺ A & B ↺ B) Each person reflects the action back onto their own body/self (or "by themselves" = alone). Ex: They looked at themselves in the mirror / We cooked dinner by ourselves.
